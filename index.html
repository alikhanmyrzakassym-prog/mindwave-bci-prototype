NON-INVASIVE BCI CONCEPT

Connecting brain signals
with visual feedback.

MindWave explores how EEG-based brain-computer interface concepts could make rehabilitation activities more engaging through simple interactive experiences.

Concept prototype · Not a medical device

Visual feedback

Our long-term research direction is to explore potential applications in rehabilitation activities for children with cerebral palsy.
